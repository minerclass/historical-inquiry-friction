Judge the choice with what he could have known.

You are a historian standing in a merchant's counting house in Boston, late June 1774. The port has been closed. A paper is waiting on his table for his signature.

Eight papers are in this room. Some belong to this moment. Others carry knowledge, words, or verdicts from later. Carry three that he could have held to the green cloth on the table, then judge his hesitation.

You have a hindsight lantern. It shows at once whether a paper belongs here, and every use adds presentist drift. Reading closely costs nothing.
